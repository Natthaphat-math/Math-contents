Rulebook›

Drawing the numbers. Every digit comes from 0–9. Zero can show up at most once. Any other digit can repeat, but never more than twice.

valid   2 1 2 7   ·   invalid   2 1 2 2 (three 2s)  ·   invalid   0 0 5 4 (two 0s)
L1Core operations

Addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and exponents. Combine all the dealt numbers, each used exactly once.

L2+ nth roots

Everything from Level 1, plus the principal nth root — only when it lands on a whole number. The root's index must be one of your dealt numbers, except the square root, which is free to use without spending a "2". Roots can't nest inside other roots at this level.

L3+ factorials & summation

Everything from Level 2. Factorials stack as deep as you like, as long as the grouping is unambiguous — (3!)! = 720. Roots may now nest two layers deep. Summation (Σ) sweeps i across a range built from your dealt numbers.

Solver note: the auto-solver searches root chains up to two layers, factorial chains up to two deep, and six summation patterns — Σi, Σ(i+i), Σ(i×i), Σiⁱ, Σi^(i!) and Σ(i!/i) — with both bounds built from any expression in your numbers.

Numbers are dealt at random within the rules above. If no exact route exists, the solver shows the closest one it found.
