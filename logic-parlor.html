How to Play

Every card is a claim about the table's current value, written as value → connective → result. Your job is to know whether the claim is true.

  • ∧ AND — true only when both sides are true
  • ∨ OR — true when at least one side is true
  • → IF…THEN — false only when the front is true and the back is false
  • ↔ IFF — true when both sides match
  • ~ NOT — flips the table's value
  • T / F — rare, gold-edged. Always playable, sets the table directly.
  1. The table starts showing a value, T or F.
  2. On your turn, drag a card onto the felt — or tap a card to lift it, then tap the felt to play it.
  3. Your card is read as "table value, your connective, ≡ your result." If that's true, it's a correct play, and the table updates to your result.
  4. If it's false, the table still updates to your stated result — but you draw two penalty cards.
  5. T and F cards need no justification: play them anytime to set the table.
  6. Don't like your hand? Tap the draw pile to draw a card and pass the turn.
  7. First to empty their hand wins the duel.
